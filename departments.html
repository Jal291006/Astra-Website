02

Technical Departments

01

Observational Astronomy

THE NIGHT SKY Head: Jainil  |  Co-Head: Jal

Focuses on observing the night sky firsthand. We organize campus stargazing sessions, learn how to assemble and align telescopes, identify constellations and planets, and practice beginner astrophotography using phones and cameras.

Activities Stargazing · Telescopes · Moon & Planetary · Astrophotography
·
02

Astrophysics & Research

THE BIG QUESTIONS Head: Aditya  |  Co-Head: Mridul

A learning and discussion group for students interested in the science behind the cosmos. We discuss concepts like Hubble’s law, black holes, and planetary motion, review interesting beginner research papers together, and hold informal peer-learning sessions.

Activities Discussion Circles · Cosmology Basics · Planetary Motion · Papers
·
03

Space Data & Software

SCIENCE THROUGH CODE Head: Yash  |  Co-Head: —

Combines programming with space science. We learn to work with open-source astronomy data from NASA and ISRO, write Python scripts to plot orbital data, explore astronomical packages like AstroPy, and build simple web pages and tools for the club.

Activities Python (AstroPy/NumPy) · Orbit Sims · Open Space Data · Web Tools
·
04

Astro-Tech & Instrumentation

HANDS-ON BUILDING Head: Aryan  |  Co-Head: Pratik

For students who enjoy electronics, hardware, and tinkering. We learn how optical lenses and sensors work, experiment with microcontrollers like Arduino and ESP32 to build basic tracking circuits, and learn how observation instruments and mounts are put together.

Activities Optics & Lenses · Arduino/ESP32 · Tracking Circuits · Hardware Care
·
05

Satellite & Communication

LISTENING FROM EARTH Head: Namya  |  Co-Head: Hiren

Explores how satellites orbit the Earth and transmit signals. We learn satellite tracking basics, experiment with inexpensive RTL-SDR receivers to pick up open signals like weather satellite broadcasts, and learn the fundamentals of antennas and RF communication.

Activities Satellite Tracking · RTL-SDR Basics · Weather Images · Simple Antennas
